Welcome Call Script
🔌 Charger Not Working Charger
Call Script:
• Sir, kya charger me light aa rahi hai?
• Agar nahi aa rhi hai toh Sir, ek baar charger cable ko nikal kr dobaara connect kijiye and dusre socket me lgaiye.
• Sir, Charger ke kaale rang ke adaptor ke upar ek safed button hoga use daba ke rakhiye 10sec tak aur phir check kijiye charge me lagakar.
• Sir ek pankhe ko thoda saaf kariye uss par dhul jami ho sakti hai.
• Sir, agar koi error aa raha hai toh error code batayein.
Hinglish System Remark:
"Customer reported charger not working. Advised socket change, cable reconnect, and 10-sec white button reset. Issue unresolved, assigned ticket to F-Ops team."
🔋 Battery Not Working Battery
Call Script:
• Important!: MOSFET check karo ki woh ON hai ki nahi and kya koi critical alert hai?
• Sir, kya battery me light aa rahi hai? Agar aa rahi toh kya handle meter me bhi aa rahi hai? Agar handle meter me nahi aa rahi hai toh ek baar aap Harness wire ko nikal kar saaf karke dobaara lagaiye.
• Sir, MCB ko OFF karke ON kariye.
• Sir, charger connect karke check kijiye charging ho rahi hai ya error aa raha hai.
• Sir, 15–20 min baad MCB OFF–ON karke restart kijiye.
• Sir, agar issue solve nahi hota, main F-Ops team ko assign kar raha hoon.
Hinglish System Remark:
"Driver complained battery output not working. Verified MOSFET status, performed Harness wire clean reconnect & MCB trip restart. Problem persists, escalated to F-Ops."
📊 SOC Meter Not Working Display / CAN
Call Script:
• Sir, wire harness reconnect kijiye aur vehicle restart kijiye.
• Sir, hide button press kijiye, CAN pin remove karke dobara insert kijiye.
• Sir, agar issue continue hota hai, main F-Ops assign kar raha hoon.
Note: SOC meter voltage aur charging level dikhata hai.
Hinglish System Remark:
"SOC meter display glitch/blank issue reported. Reconnected CAN pin and harness wire, restarted vehicle. No change in status, ticket assigned to F-Ops field technician."

📞 Welcome Call Script Script
Introduction
Namaskar Sir, main Battery Smart se [Caller Name] bol raha hoon. Kya meri baat [Driver Name] se ho rahi hai?
Sir, system me check kar raha hoon ki aap recently Battery Smart ke saath onboard hue hain. Sabse pehle, aapka humare saath judne ke liye bahut-bahut dhanyavaad.
Kya abhi 2–3 minute baat karne ke liye theek samay hai?

Objective of Call
Sir, ye ek welcome call hai jisme main aapko battery aur charger ko sahi tarike se use karne ki basic information share karunga.
Isse aapko better battery range milegi aur future me issues kam aayenge. Agar beech me koi bhi doubt aaye, aap bina hesitation ke pooch sakte hain.

Battery Usage Tips
• Sir, kyunki aapne nayi battery li hai, sabse pehle ise full charge (FC) tak charge karein.
• Battery ko kabhi bhi completely discharge na hone dein — jaise hi 20% SOC aaye, charging laga dein.
• Hamesha Battery Smart ka original charger hi use karein.
• Third-party charger ya repair se bachhein, isse battery damage ho sakti hai.

Charger Usage Tips
• Charging hamesha dry aur ventilated jagah par karein.
• Charger lagate hi indicator light ON honi chahiye.
• Charger ki lead aur plug properly connected hone chahiye.
• Switch board ON hona zaroori hai.
Ek important baat — agar charger lost ya stolen ho jata hai, to company policy ke according ₹10,000 charge lagta hai.

How to Get Better Battery Range
• Battery ko hamesha 100% charge karke use karein.
• Vehicle ko overload na karein — ideal load driver + 3–4 passengers tak rakhein.
• Tyre pressure 40–45 PSI maintain rakhein.
• Vehicle ka regular maintenance karte rahein.
• Sudden acceleration aur rough driving se bachein.

MCB Handling
• Junction box ko hamesha properly cover karke rakhein.
• Charging ke time MCB OFF rakhein.

CAN Wire Check
• Battery connector wire properly connected honi chahiye.
• Meter par CAN communication light blink karni chahiye.

Common Issues & Quick Checks
1️⃣ Wire harness connection loose to nahi hai
2️⃣ MCB trip ya OFF to nahi hai
3️⃣ Battery me charge hai ya nahi
